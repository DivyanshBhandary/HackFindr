# Problem validation survey

Goal: show the judges that the problem is real, with numbers from real students, not guesses.
Aim for **30+ responses** from at least **3 colleges**, collected during the hackathon (other teams,
volunteers, friends' class groups). Put the results in the README's *Problem validation* section.

## Google Form (copy these questions)

Intro text: *"We're building HackFindr at Hackasol 5.0, a tool that finds hackathons and workshops near you and
checks if you can join. 1 minute, anonymous."*

1. **Which year are you in?** 1st / 2nd / 3rd / 4th / 5th / Postgraduate
2. **Which city is your college in?** (short answer)
3. **How many hackathons, workshops or tech events did you join in the last 12 months?** 0 / 1–2 / 3–5 / 6+
4. **Where do you find out about them?** (tick all) Unstop / Devfolio / Devpost / Instagram / WhatsApp groups /
   College notice board / Friends / Other
5. **How many different places do you check to find events?** 1 / 2–3 / 4–5 / 6+
6. **In the last year, did you miss an event you wanted to join because you found out too late?** Yes / No
7. **Have you ever started registering and then found out you weren't eligible** (wrong year, branch, state,
   team size)? Yes, more than once / Yes, once / No
8. **About how long does it take you to find one event that suits you and that you can join?**
   Under 5 min / 5–15 min / 15–30 min / More than 30 min
9. **Which would you use?** (tick all) Map of events near me / "Can I join?" check / Ranking by my skills /
   Deadline reminder emails / Add an event from a poster photo
10. **Anything else that annoys you about finding events?** (long answer, optional)
11. *(optional)* **Can we quote your answer (without your name)?** Yes / No

## What to put in the README

Fill in from the form's *Responses → Summary* tab:

- "**N** students from **K** colleges answered."
- "**X%** check 4 or more places to find events." (Q5)
- "**Y%** missed an event because they found out too late." (Q6)
- "**Z%** started registering for an event they weren't eligible for." (Q7)
- "Median time to find one suitable event: **T minutes**." (Q8)
- The top 2 features students picked (Q9). Compare them with what HackFindr has.
- 2–3 short quotes from Q10, only from people who said yes to Q11.

Only use numbers you actually collected. If you have fewer than 30 responses, say so.
